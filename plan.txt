# Elektrolab — Stage 1 plan (aligned to the class notes)

## Context
Study app for Czech gymnázium electrostatics. Physics is shown in 3D instead of written out.
It will be **presented to the class and the teacher**, so everything in it must match what was
taught: the same symbols, terms and formulas, and examples taken from the lessons. Nothing from
outside the notes appears unless it is clearly marked.
Plain HTML/CSS/JS + Three.js from CDN, no build, GitHub Pages, iPhone touch. Physics code is
commented so the user can learn from it. Five stages in total; **this plan builds Stage 1 only**
and records the class-alignment rules for every later stage.

Triage: medium · tests yes (pure physics in Node) · no branch (new project, no repo yet).

Source notes (read 2026-10-20): `Downloads/01 Elektrický náboj.pdf`, `02 Coulombův zákon.pdf`,
`03 Permitivita prostředí.pdf`, `04 Elektrické pole.pdf`, `05 Práce v elektrickém poli.pdf`,
`06 Elektrický potenciál.pdf`, plus the user's own handwritten class notes `Coulumbův Zákon.pdf`
(what was actually written on the board). **Where the notes and the textbook PDFs differ, the
user's class notes win.**

## Class-alignment rules (apply to all stages)
- **Symbols, from the class notes:** Q (Q₁, Q₂, Q₀ for the test charge), **r** for distance (the
  board used r, the textbook R), F_e for the electric force, E, W, U, U_AB, φ, Ep, d, s, α.
- **Main form of Coulomb's law, as on the board:** F_e = 1/(4π·ε) · Q₁·Q₂/r², with ε = ε₀·εᵣ
  ("představuje prostředí – permitivita"). The k form F = k·Q₁·Q₂/r² with k = 9·10⁹ N·m²·C⁻² is
  shown alongside it as the vacuum/air shortcut (lesson 02/03).
- **Constants as in the notes:** ε₀ = 8,85·10⁻¹² C²·N⁻¹·m⁻²; e = 1,602·10⁻¹⁹ C. Results are
  shown to 2–3 significant figures, so they match the sešit (e.g. 3,6·10⁶ N).
- **Intenzita units:** V·m⁻¹ is primary (class notes: "E [V/m]"), with N·C⁻¹ as the equal unit
  from lesson 04. Intro line from the notes: E = F/Q.
- **Number format:** decimal comma, "·10ⁿ" (centre dot, not ×), units such as N·C⁻¹ and V·m⁻¹.
- **Formulas, only these:** F_e = 1/(4π·ε)·Q₁·Q₂/r² (= k·Q₁·Q₂/r²), |E| = k·|Q|/r² (point charge), F = Q·E,
  W = F·s·cos α, W = Q·U, U = E·d (homogeneous field), U = W/Q, φ = Ep/Q,
  U_AB = φ_A − φ_B (the work the field does on 1 C moving from A to B). Magnitudes plus
  direction words (přitažlivá / odpudivá). No vector components.
- **Potential: visual only (user's choice).** No φ = k·Q/r formula anywhere. φ appears as colour
  and height (the "3D kopec" from lesson 06) plus readings. Earth is the zero level, as in the
  notes. Internally φ is still computed by superposition (code comment only).
- **Terms:** siločára = "čára, po které by se pohyboval jednotkový kladný náboj"; radiální pole,
  pole dipólu, homogenní pole (dvě nabité desky); ekvipotenciální plochy ⟂ siločáry; konzervativní
  síly; elementární náboj; zákon zachování náboje.
- **Presets come from the lessons, not invented:** elektron (−e), proton (+e), 1 µC
  (05 Př. 6), 2 µC (04 Př. 2), skleněná tyč (+) and novodurová tyč (−) (01, magnitude marked
  "přibližně"). The balloon is dropped because it is not in the notes.
- **Prostředí (lesson 03):** k = 1/(4π·ε₀·εᵣ). k = 9·10⁹ "platí pro vakuum a přibližně také pro
  vzduch". The selector uses only lesson 03's table values: vakuum 1, vzduch 1,0006, petrolej 2,0,
  porcelán 6, ethanol 24, voda 81. Plus a free εᵣ slider (1–100) for 03 Př. 5 (εᵣ = 2 → half the
  force) and Př. 8 (εᵣ ≈ 3,5). Range-only materials (papír 2–2,5, sklo 5–10) are left out so no
  value is invented. Readouts show the current k (e.g. ethanol → 3,75·10⁸ N·m²·C⁻²).
- **Units:** nC and µC as in the notes (150 nC, 1 µC).

## Stage roadmap (adjusted to the notes)
1. **Coulombův zákon** (this plan): charges, dragging, force arrows, siločáry, readouts, status line.
2. **Elektrické pole:** vector grid of E, colour on a log scale with legend (lesson 04 notes that
   colour, not length, shows strength). Scenes: radiální / dipól / **dvě nabité desky**.
3. **Potenciál:** colour slice, ekvipotenciální plochy, and the 3D height surface ("strmost kopce" =
   E), all visual, no formula. It follows the class-notes storyline: the gravity analogy first
   (lifted stone, W = ΔEp = F·h = m·g·h, gravitační intenzita F/m, potenciál Ep/m), then
   "vektorový vs. skalární popis pole", vrstevnice on a map with the **gradient** (the term from
   the notes), and finally the charged plates with A, B and equipotential lines parallel to the
   plates, φ [V], U_AB = φ_A − φ_B.
4. **Práce a napětí:** test charge from A to B, W = Q·U_AB, voltmeter, φ(x) graph; different
   paths give the same W (the homogeneous-field staircase from lesson 06); W > 0, < 0 and = 0
   cases (05 Př. 6). Class-notes chain on screen: E = F/Q → F = E·Q → W = F·d → W = Q·E·d →
   U = E·d → W = Q·U_AB. Socket example as done in class: U = 230 V, d = 0,02 m →
   E = 11 500 V·m⁻¹ (the textbook uses 1,8 cm → 12 800).
5. **Lekce:** short Czech panels per mode in KaTeX, the "Závěrečný přehled" table (F/E, Ep/φ, W/U)
   as the summary, and "Příklad ze sešitu" buttons.

## Stage 1 — behaviour
- **Units:** scene unit = 1 m. The bench has two scales, **4 m** (0,5 m grid) and **40 cm**
  (5 cm grid), so the lesson examples fit at true scale (0,5–2 m in 02/04; 3,4 cm and 10 cm in 03).
  Each sešit example picks its scale. r is clamped at a minimum (sphere radius) and the code says
  why.
- **Prostředí selector** (left panel) and the current k/εᵣ in "Měření". Changing the medium
  re-renders the arrows, and the status line says e.g. "V ethanolu je síla 24krát menší než ve
  vakuu."
- **"Dotkni se" (touch the charges):** for 03 Př. 7, charges 1 and 2 touch and share their total,
  then return to their positions. (+80 nC, −20 nC) → 30 nC each, and attraction flips to
  repulsion. The status line cites zákon zachování elektrického náboje.
- **Layout:** top bar with the status sentence · left "Ovládání" (přidat +/−, presets, Q slider with
  exact preset values, r slider between charges 1–2, smazat) · centre "Obr. 1" dark viewport with
  Šikmo / Z boku / Shora · right "Měření" (F_e, r, Q₁, Q₂, |E| at the selected charge, přitažlivá /
  odpudivá). Phone: viewport first, panels below, 16 px gutters, no horizontal scroll.
- **Příklad ze sešitu (Stage 1 set):** 02 Př. 1 (0,01 C, 0,01 C, 0,5 m → 3,6·10⁶ N); 02 Př. 3
  (3,3·10⁻⁴ C at 1 m → 1000 N); 03 Př. 1 (150 nC, 100 nC, 0,034 m, vakuum → 0,12 N); 03 Př. 6
  (same, ethanol → 4,9·10⁻³ N); 03 Př. 7 (+80 nC, −20 nC, 0,1 m → 1,44·10⁻³ N, then "Dotkni se" →
  8,1·10⁻⁴ N). One click sets up the scene so the readout shows the sešit result.
  (The electron example at 10⁻⁹ m is out of bench scale. It moves to Stage 5 lessons.)
- **Drag:** pointer events (mouse + touch), raycast onto the bench plane, OrbitControls off while
  dragging, `touch-action: none`. Tap to select. Max 6 charges.
- **Force arrows:** the net Coulomb force on each charge, with length on a log scale so e and µC
  are both visible. The true value is in the readouts, and a caption says length is not to scale.
- **Siločáry:** traced in 3D from + toward −, with arrowheads in the direction a jednotkový kladný
  náboj would move. Retraced on change (coarse while dragging, full detail on release). Single
  colour; log colouring comes in Stage 2.
- **Status line** uses wording from the notes: "Náboje stejného znaménka se odpuzují.",
  "Náboje opačného znaménka se přitahují.", "Elektrická síla s rostoucí vzdáleností klesá — náboj
  je daleko, síla je téměř nulová.", "Vzdálenost se zmenšila na polovinu — síla vzrostla
  čtyřikrát.", "Přidej náboj tlačítkem vlevo."
- **Design:** paper panels (#f4efe4-ish) around a near-black viewport, "Obr. 1" labels, Source
  Serif 4 headings + IBM Plex Sans/Mono numbers (Google Fonts, free), colour tokens in CSS.

## Stage 2 — Elektrické pole (detailed plan, started 2026-10-21)

Source: lesson 04 + class notes ("E = F/Q", "E [V/m]", radiální pole má střed, siločáry,
pole homogenní). Key teaching point from 04: in the vector pictures the field strength is shown
by COLOUR, not arrow length ("světlejší barva znamená silnější pole").

- Mode tabs in the top bar: "1 · Coulombův zákon" | "2 · Elektrické pole". Mode sets display
  defaults (mode 2: vectors on), the example list and the Měření sections. Everything else is shared.
- Vector grid of E (core/fieldgrid.js): arrows of EQUAL length at grid points, coloured by
  log10|E| (core/colormap.js, dark → light = weak → strong). "v rovině" (y = 0, default) or
  "v prostoru" (3 layers), or hidden. Points inside charges / plates are skipped.
- Field lines are coloured with the same log scale. Legend in the viewport: gradient bar with
  decade ticks in V·m⁻¹ and "světlejší = silnější pole". The colour range is fixed per scene
  (computed in vacuum when the set of charges/plates/scale changes), so switching the medium
  visibly darkens the field instead of re-normalising it away.
- Typ pole presets: radiální (one +), dipól (+ and −), dvě nabité desky (homogenní pole).
  Plates = two parallel square sheets standing on the bench (core/plates.js), modelled as a grid
  of point charges (superposition); field lines start/end on plate faces and stop when they cross
  a plate. Plate charge ±5 µC (a scene value, not a taught number — shown as such).
- Bod P (probe), mode 2 only: draggable marker; Měření shows E in P in V·m⁻¹ = N·C⁻¹ and
  "na náboj 1 C by tu působila síla … N" (the lesson-04 definition of intenzita).
- Měření in mode 2: E = F/Q (class notes), |E| = k·|Q|/r² (point charge, as in 04),
  F = Q·E with live substitution for the selected charge.
- Status sentences (mode 2): radial / dipole / homogeneous descriptions in the lesson's words,
  the probe reading, "test charge between plates → same force everywhere", and the medium change
  phrased for intenzita.
- Example 04 Př. 1: Q₀ = 10⁻³ C, Q₁ = 1 C, 2 m → E = 2,25·10⁶ V·m⁻¹, F = 2 250 000 N. Charges keep
  their textbook labels (Q₀, Q₁). Changing Q₁ (variants b–d) keeps the example valid: E stays the
  same, only F changes — that is the lesson's point.
- Tests: plates (uniform between plates, direction + → −, weaker outside), field grid (counts,
  skipping), colormap (range in whole decades, monotonic), status sentences, example 04-1.
- Verify in Chrome: each preset, probe drag, medium change darkens colours, phone width.
- Status 2026-10-21: Stage 2 built. 51/51 tests. Verified in Chrome: presets, probe drag, 04 Př. 1
  a)–d), medium darkening, plates homogeneity, phone width. Changes vs plan: plates are
  0,5·S wide with a 0,25·S gap (wider than the gap so the field inside is homogeneous); the
  "same force everywhere" sentence only fires in the central half of the gap (edges differ ~7 %).

  elektrické pole, intenzita, práce v poli;
